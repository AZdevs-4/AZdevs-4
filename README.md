### 📈 [E-Commerce Revenue Architecture & Margin Optimization](https://github.com/AZdevs-4/ecommerce-revenue-margin-optimization)
`Power BI` | `DAX` | `T-SQL` | `Star Schema` | `Dimensional Modeling`

*   **The Business Problem:** The enterprise was suffering from bottom-line profit compression across localized hubs (including Karachi and Lahore) despite maintaining a healthy sales volume of **$79.79M in Net Revenue** due to unmonitored promotional discounting ($4.13M) and hidden logistics costs ($1.25M).

*   **Technical Implementation:** Refactored a flat transactional sheet into an optimized **Star Schema** (`1:*` relationships connecting dynamic customer, geography, calendar, and product tables to a central Sales Fact table). Authored robust DAX measures to track cumulative values and margin alerts, and integrated an AI-powered natural language narrative block for instant automated performance reporting.

### 🗄️ [Enterprise Database Refactoring & Schema Optimization](https://github.com/AZdevs-4/database-refactoring-schema-optimization)
`T-SQL` | `SQL Server` | `Schema Normalization` | `CTEs` | `Window Functions`

*   **The Problem:** Raw, flat housing transactional databases frequently store unstructured, nested text and redundant duplicated keys that inflate query runtimes, skew downstream BI metrics, and waste production warehouse storage.
*   **The Technical Solution:** Engineered a clean, optimized relational schema from the ground up. Used `SUBSTRING`, `CHARINDEX`, and `PARSENAME` to parse messy geographic strings into atomic dimension fields (Address, City, State). Resolved duplicate transaction anomalies by deploying window-partitioning logic (`ROW_NUMBER() OVER(PARTITION BY...)`) inside Common Table Expressions (CTEs) to safely purge duplicate records without compromising primary relational key integrity.
